# Analytics plan

Do not add analytics merely because it exists.

## Pre-launch events worth measuring
- product_page_view
- discovery_trio_view
- compare_view
- guide_view
- market_page_view
- trade_page_view
- enquiry_submit_success
- enquiry_fallback_mailto

## Launch events
- add_to_cart
- begin_checkout
- purchase
- shipping_country
- bundle_vs_single
- first_order_aov

## Primary commercial metric
Contribution margin per order after customer acquisition cost.

## Privacy
Any non-essential analytics or advertising technology must be reviewed against the privacy/cookie policy before activation.
