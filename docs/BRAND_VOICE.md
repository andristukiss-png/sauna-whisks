# Brand voice

## Position
A serious Latvia-based specialist company: botanical, informed and commercially modern.

Sauna whisks are the hero category, but the brand can grow into the wider sauna ritual rather than presenting itself as a single-product novelty shop.

## Category language
Use **sauna whisk** as the primary English category term.

Use these as supporting terminology where context helps:
- sauna broom — secondary English SEO synonym
- venik — banya / Eastern European terminology
- vihta / vasta — Finnish terminology

Do not stuff all synonyms into every product title. Explain them naturally in education, glossary, comparison and metadata copy.

## Write like
- specific
- calm
- knowledgeable
- evidence-aware
- respectful of regional differences
- confident without invented authority
- clear enough for a US customer encountering the category for the first time

## Avoid
- fake centuries-old company heritage
- mystical wellness clichés
- unsupported medical claims
- "detox" claims
- pretending pirts, Finnish sauna and banya are identical
- vague "premium quality" without explaining the quality dimension
- aggressive luxury language for an agricultural/natural product
- marketplace-style keyword stuffing
- reducing the ritual to "hitting yourself with branches"

## Preferred vocabulary
Sauna whisk, birch sauna whisk, oak sauna whisk, eucalyptus sauna whisk, sauna broom, venik, vihta, vasta, pirts, harvest, origin, preparation, leaf retention, producer, condition, batch, steam, heat, botanical aroma.

## Product storytelling
Use material + verified origin + condition + harvest + producer + technique.

Prefer concise product naming such as:
- Latvian Birch Sauna Whisk
- Latvian Oak Sauna Whisk
- Eucalyptus Sauna Whisk
- Birch & Oak Sauna Whisk Set
- Sauna Whisk Variety Pack

Use **Latvian**, **hand-tied**, species, harvest and producer claims only when verified for the exact SKU.

## Customer framing
Prefer language around traditional sauna massage/body work, botanical aroma, steam and heat ritual, preparation, material feel and Northern European sauna traditions.

Aroma and ritual language must not become unsupported medical, therapeutic or detox claims.
