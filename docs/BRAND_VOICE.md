# Brand voice

## Position
A serious Latvia-based specialist company: botanical, informed and commercially modern.

## Write like
- specific
- calm
- knowledgeable
- evidence-aware
- respectful of regional differences
- confident without invented authority

## Avoid
- fake centuries-old company heritage
- mystical wellness clichés
- unsupported medical claims
- "detox" claims
- pretending pirts, Finnish sauna and banya are identical
- vague "premium quality" without explaining the quality dimension
- aggressive luxury language for an agricultural/natural product

## Preferred vocabulary
Sauna whisk, birch, oak, eucalyptus, venik, vihta, vasta, pirts, harvest, origin, preparation, leaf retention, producer, condition, batch.

## Product storytelling
Use material + origin + condition + harvest + producer + technique.
