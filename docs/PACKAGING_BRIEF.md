# Packaging brief

Packaging must protect a fragile natural product without making shipping uneconomic.

## Functional
- reduce crushing
- protect from uncontrolled moisture
- maintain tying/handle shape
- support 1-, 2- and 3-whisk orders
- survive parcel shipping

## Customer information
Include:
- product/material
- origin when verified
- condition
- batch/lot field where used
- preparation
- storage
- natural-variation note
- contact / website

## Sustainability
Avoid making environmental claims that cannot be substantiated.
