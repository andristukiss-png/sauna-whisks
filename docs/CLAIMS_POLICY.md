# Claims policy

SaunaWhisks.com should describe observable product characteristics and documented tradition without making unsupported health claims.

## Allowed categories
- material / botanical identity when verified
- origin / harvest when documented
- physical feel
- aroma character
- dimensions / weight
- preservation condition
- preparation and storage
- leaf retention / testing observations
- cultural or historical context with sources

## Avoid
- detox / detoxification claims
- treating or curing disease
- pain-relief claims without appropriate evidence/regulatory review
- immunity claims
- circulation claims presented as medical benefits
- mental-health claims
- claims that a plant's aroma has a medical effect
- "ancient healing" as unsourced marketing language

If a future claim could reasonably influence a health decision, it requires a separate evidence and regulatory review before publication.
