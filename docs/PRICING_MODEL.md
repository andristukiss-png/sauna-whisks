# Pricing model

Final retail price should start from landed economics, not competitor screenshots.

## Per-unit landed cost
- supplier unit cost
- inbound freight allocation
- import/brokerage where applicable
- packaging
- pick/pack
- payment processing allocation
- expected damage/replacement allowance

## Order economics
Model:
- average order value
- shipping collected vs actual shipping cost
- contribution margin before CAC
- CAC
- contribution margin after CAC
- bundle mix
- repeat rate

## Rule
A high gross-margin percentage is not sufficient if shipping and acquisition absorb the contribution.

## US market anchor — 2026-09-24

A dated public-market snapshot is recorded in `docs/US_MARKET_RESEARCH.md`.

Observed specialist single-whisk examples ranged from about **US$14.90 to US$28**, with selected marketplace examples spanning a much wider range. Current planned SaunaWhisks.com single prices sit near the upper half of the specialist examples observed.

This is context, not validation. Do not change launch prices from competitor screenshots alone.

A higher price than low-cost specialist listings must be supported by real customer value such as verified provenance, documented quality, protective packaging, preparation guidance, service, or better bundle economics.

Before final price approval, calculate contribution after:
- landed unit cost
- packaging
- fulfilment
- outbound shipping subsidy
- payment fees
- replacement / damage allowance
- CAC
- returns / support allowance where relevant

Then stress-test singles, bundles and trade cases separately.
