# Supplier onboarding

## Stage 1 — Initial fit
Collect:
- company / producer identity
- production location
- harvest location
- materials/species
- preservation methods
- MOQ
- capacity
- lead time
- export history

## Stage 2 — Samples
Request representative samples and document:
- sample/batch reference
- dimensions/weight
- tying/handle construction
- preparation method
- leaf retention
- flexibility
- packaging condition
- aroma character

## Stage 3 — Commercial
Confirm:
- unit price
- packaging options
- payment terms
- production calendar
- export documents
- replacement/damage policy

## Stage 4 — SKU approval
Do not publish precise origin/harvest/producer claims until evidence is retained internally.
